1. **Financial Independence & Agency:**
   - Building a career path that guarantees financial security, personal freedom, and autonomy over my time and decisions.
2. **Supporting My Family:**
   - Constructing a foundation stable enough to provide for, support, and lift up those who invested in my early years.
3. **Creation & Shipping:**
   - Shifting from a consumer of technology to a creator who designs and ships functional, scalable AI systems that positively affect people's daily workflows.
4. **Keeping My Promises:**
   - Honoring the commitment I made to myself to not settle for average or look back on university with regret.
5. **Architecting the Future:**
   - Gaining the skill set required to actively collaborate with and build the future alongside high-agency people I love and respect.
