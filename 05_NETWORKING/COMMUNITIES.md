# Tech Communities & Meetups

Active participation in local and global tech ecosystems is a massive shortcut to peer learning and job matching.

---

## 📍 Local Delhi NCR & Gurgaon Hubs

1. **PyData Delhi / Gurgaon Chapter**
   - **Focus:** Data science, classical machine learning, PyTorch, core Python ecosystems.
   - **Action:** Monitor event portals for weekend meetups in Gurgaon Cyber City or Delhi.
2. **Google Developer Groups (GDG) Gurgaon**
   - **Focus:** Broad software engineering, cloud, Google AI platforms.
   - **Action:** Participate in DevFests and monthly local tracks.
3. **Delhi/NCR AI Saturdays (AI6)**
   - **Focus:** Structured study circles for deep learning and reinforcement learning.
   - **Action:** Apply for active learning cohorts.
4. **Local University Hackathons**
   - **Focus:** Inter-college product creation. Target events at IILM, DU, DTU, and NSUT.

---

## 🌐 Global & Online Communities

* **Hugging Face Forums & Discord:** Pre-eminent community for open-source AI and model sharing.
* **Kaggle:** Excellent for feature engineering, data exploration techniques, and structured datasets.
* **Major Open Source Discord Servers:** LangChain, LangGraph, vLLM, and PyTorch channels.
* **MLH (Major League Hacking):** Build hacks, engage with global developer sprints.
