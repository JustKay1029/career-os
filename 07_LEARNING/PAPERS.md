## 📑 How to Read an AI Paper (Template)

Copy this template for each paper you review:

```markdown
### [Paper Title]

* **Link:**
* **Core Hypothesis:** *What problem is the paper attempting to solve?*
* **Key Innovations:** *What new architectures, loss functions, or data pipelines are introduced?*
* **How it works:** *Detail the math/mechanism (e.g., self-attention, layer normalization).*
* **Key Benchmarks & Results:** *How did it perform compared to baselines?*
* **Takeaway for my projects:** *How does this apply to the systems I am building?*
```
